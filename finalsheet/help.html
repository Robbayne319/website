Game day in 10 steps

  1. On the Game tab, type the opponent. Tap Home/Away, and add location, weather and officials if you like.
  2. Tap players to pick your starters, then Put on field. Tap Mark player out for anyone sick, injured or unavailable.
  3. Set up the field: drag players into position, or tap a spot to choose who plays there.
  4. At the opening whistle, tap Kick off. It unlocks once exactly 11 are on the field.
  5. When the ref stops play, tap Stop, then a reason. Tap Resume when play restarts.
  6. For a sub, tap Sub, tap who's coming off, then who's coming on, then SUB.
  7. Record goals with + Goal under each score (yours or theirs): scorer, then assist. Record a yellow or red with Card.
  8. The half ends on its own at 0:00. Tap Start for the 2nd half.
  9. At the final whistle, tap Finish. Never use Discard to end a game.
  10. Tap Game report (PDF) or Share graphic and send it. For the next game, tap Start new game.

Roster

  • Player photos: most players are minors, so get permission from players or parents first and follow your school's or club's media policy. Photos stay on your phone and, on a shared team, are visible only to its members.
  • Enter a number and name, then tap Add player.
  • Edit a number or name right in the list. Changes save when you leave the box.
  • Tap the circle next to a player to take or choose a photo. It shows on the roster, the field view and goal graphics. Player photos are part of FinalSheet Pro.
  • Import list: paste a whole roster, one player per line with a name and number in either order ("Sam Rivera, 11", "11 Sam Rivera", "#11 Sam Rivera"), copy Name and Number columns from a spreadsheet, or tap Choose CSV file to open one from Files, iCloud Drive or an email. Check the preview: coaches and managers, duplicates and players already on the roster are left out, and a new number for an existing player updates it.
  • Usual position: tap a player and pick GK, D, M or F (or Any). It shows on the roster and on the subs board, so you can see who can replace whom.
  • You can't remove a player who has minutes in the current game.
  • Players added mid-game show up on the bench right away.

Before kickoff

  • Tap players to select your starters, then tap Put on field in the bar at the bottom.
  • Kick off unlocks once exactly the right number are on the field.
  • Mark player out for anyone sick, injured or unavailable. That game won't count against their season percentages.
  • Fill in opponent, date, home/away, location, weather, field and officials. Officials print at the top of the report.
  • Half length here applies to this game only.

Field and positions

  • The Field view shows your formation with each player in their spot, with number and minutes.
  • Pick a formation from the chips above the field. Choices match your players-on-field setting.
  • Drag a player onto another spot to move them there. Drop them on a teammate and the two swap.
  • Tap a spot to choose who plays there. Players who already have a spot are greyed out. To swap two players, drag one onto the other.
  • On a sub, the player coming on takes the spot of the player going off (in the order you tapped them).
  • Changing formation keeps players in the same position where the new shape has it.
  • Positions show in the player list and on the report next to each name. Tap the Field heading to hide it.

The clock

  • The big button starts, stops and resumes the clock.
  • When you stop the clock, tap a reason (Injury, Goal, Card...) to log why.
  • The half ends on its own at 0:00, even if your phone was locked.
  • To end a half early, tap End 1st Half (or End 2nd Half).
  • Adjust clock (under the time) matches the field clock. The buttons open right under the clock so you can watch it change. While it runs: −5s, −1s, +1s, +5s, and it keeps running. While stopped: seconds or minutes, and it shows what the clock was and how far you moved it. The buttons move the clock as shown, so + adds time on a countdown clock. Tap Done to close them.
  • Count down or count up is set in Settings.

Substitutions

  • During a game, tap Sub to open the subs board: everyone on the field and the whole bench as jersey numbers, each with their position and minutes.
  • Tap who's coming off, then who's coming on, then SUB. Off and on pair up in the order you tap them, so you can make several subs at once. They're logged together as one sub.
  • The bench filters by position (All, GK, D, M, F) and sorts by number or fewest minutes (⇅). After you tap a player coming off, the board shows who you need, like Need a D for #4.
  • Big roster? Tap # and type a jersey number to pick a bench player.
  • Clear starts over. SUB stays grey until the field count adds up, and the bar says what's wrong.
  • A player's position comes from their spot on the field, else their usual position on the roster, else the spot they last played.
  • Before kickoff, Sub goes to the lineup list instead: tap players, then Put on field.
  • Minutes are tracked to the second for every player. A sent-off player can't come back on.

Goals

  • Tap + Goal under your score. Pick Regular, Penalty kick or Own goal, tap the scorer's number (or Unknown), then the assist (or None), and Save goal.
  • Only players on the field can score or assist.
  • Tap + Goal under the opponent's score for their goals. With Confirm opponent goals on (Settings), it asks Goal or PK goal first, so a stray tap doesn't count.
  • Got the scorer or assist wrong? Tap the goal, in the list or on the timeline, to change it.
  • Press and hold a goal in the list to remove it.
  • Got video of the goal? See Goal clips and highlight reels.

Cards

  • Tap Card on the scoreboard, pick the player (yours or theirs), the color and a reason.
  • A second yellow records a red automatically and sends the player off. Your team plays one short.
  • Don't know the reason yet? Save the card without one. It shows in red as Reason not recorded.
  • Tap a card in the list (or in the game log) to fill in or fix the reason, note or their jersey number, even after the game is finished.
  • To remove a card, tap it and choose Remove this card.

Timeline

  • The bar at the bottom of the Game tab shows the whole game: each half, every sub (thin line), goals (green for us, red for them) and cards.
  • Tap a mark to see the minute, who was involved and the score at the time. Tap Edit to fix a goal or card.
  • The same timeline is in each game on the Season tab and near the top of the PDF report.

Mistakes and undo

  • Undo on the scoreboard reverses the last change: subs, goals, cards, clock actions.
  • Fix a sub later: open the Game log (or tap a sub mark on the timeline, then Edit) and tap the sub. Change when it happened, who went off or came on, or delete it. Minutes, the field and the report are worked out again. The app won't save a change that doesn't add up (someone not on the field then, or the wrong number on the field). Works for games started after this update.
  • Game info edits and stop reasons aren't part of undo. Just change them again.
  • Discard game at the bottom deletes the whole game without saving it.

Overtime and shootouts

  • After the 2nd half ends, tap Add OT under the scoreboard for an overtime period (length set in Settings).
  • If it is tied, tap Penalty shootout. Best of 5, then sudden death one round at a time for as long as it takes.
  • Kicks alternate. A team that is a kick ahead waits for the other.
  • Players who have kicked are greyed out until every eligible teammate has taken one. Then everyone can kick again, in turn.
  • Only players on the field at the final whistle can take a kick.
  • Shootout kicks don't count as goals or in the score. The report shows the shootout result.

Schedule

  • Season › Add game puts a game on the schedule: opponent, home or away, date, kickoff, location and a note. Home games fill in your home field (the first one you enter is remembered).
  • The schedule is shared with the team, so every phone sees it. Tap a game to change it or remove it.
  • Before a game, the Game tab shows the next game on the schedule. Start game fills in the setup (opponent, date, kickoff, home/away, location); then pick your lineup and kick off. Not this game? starts a blank one instead.
  • Set up game on the Season tab does the same from there. The pin button opens directions in Maps.
  • Game-day graphics: the share button on the next game (Season tab), Post a game-day graphic on the Game tab, or Game-day graphic in the game setup. Game day shows the date big with the opponent, kickoff and place; Game day · player features one player (pick who) with their photo over their number. Both come in story and feed sizes, in your brand kit.
  • Once the game is finished and saved, it leaves the schedule and shows under Results.

Finishing and reports

  • If the season already has a game against the same opponent on the same date (say, another parent on your team saved it), Finish asks whether to replace that game or keep both, so it isn't counted twice.
  • At full time, tap the big Finish & save game tile under the scoreboard. The game is frozen and added to your season.
  • Game report (PDF) makes a printable report to text, email or save to Files.
  • Share report text sends the same report as plain text. It ends with a short recap ready to paste into a post: the final score, scorers and assists by last name, and your team's tags (Settings › your team › Social posts).
  • You can share a Report so far during the game too.
  • Free version: Finish ends the game and keeps the score, timeline and graphics on screen until you start a new game. Saving games to your season and game reports (PDF, text, CSV) are part of FinalSheet Pro (Settings > FinalSheet Pro).
  • On someone else's team, reports and the season follow your role there: that team's Manager has Pro.

Goal clips and highlight reels

  • Film the play with your phone's Camera app, like you always do. FinalSheet doesn't need to be open or recording.
  • After you log the goal, tap it, then Choose video and pick the clip (up to 30 seconds).
  • iPhone: The iPhone picker lets you trim the video to the goal right there.
  • Android: Longer than 30 seconds? Trim it in Google Photos (or your gallery app) first, then pick it.
  • Clips stay on this phone. They aren't uploaded or shared with your team: other phones see that the goal has a clip, but not the video.
  • Big videos are saved at 1080p to save space. That still looks sharp on Instagram and YouTube.
  • On the goal: Play, Share, Replace or Remove the clip. Share asks With sound or No sound.
  • Highlight reel: after the game, open it on the Season tab and tap Make wide 16:9 (YouTube, Facebook) or Make story 9:16 (Instagram, TikTok). Pick Keep sound or No sound (silent, so you can add music when you post).
  • Each goal gets a title banner with the minute, scorer and score, and the reel ends on a final-score card in your team colors. Nothing gets cropped: a blurred copy of the clip fills any gap.
  • Free up space: once a reel is made, the reel card offers to delete that game's goal clips (the reel keeps them). You can't remake the reel afterward, so make both shapes first if you want both.
  • Deleting a game deletes its clips and reels too. See Settings › Storage for how much space videos use.

Share graphics

  • Tap Share graphic (social media) on the Game tab, or Graphic on any game in the Season tab. The editor opens full screen: preview in the middle, four tool tabs underneath.
  • Graphic tab: pick Final score, Live score, Goal or Lineup (only the ones that fit the game right now) and Story 9:16 or Feed 4:5. For a goal, pick which goal and the player photo.
  • iPhone: Tap Share or save image at the bottom. In the share menu, tap Save Image to keep it in Photos, or send it straight to Instagram.
  • Android: Tap Share or save image at the bottom. In the share menu, pick Google Photos or Drive to keep a copy, or send it straight to Instagram.
  • Done closes the editor. Your changes stay until you leave; save them to a kit to keep them for next time.
  • The FinalSheet logo stays on every graphic. You can move it and make it bigger.
  • Free version: graphics use the standard FinalSheet look. Style, Photo and Layers (team colors, brand kits, sponsors, background photos, cutouts and moving pieces) are part of FinalSheet Pro.
  • Followers (parents on a Pro team): graphics use the team's look as the Manager set it up. Pick the graphic and size, and add your own photo to a goal graphic. Styling and moving pieces are for the Manager and Scorekeepers.

Designing graphics

  • Style tab: a brand kit is a saved look with colors, text color, headline font, sponsor logos and saved layouts. Tap Save as new kit to keep the current look under a new name (say, Playoffs), and Edit kit to change one. To delete a kit, select it and tap Delete (you always keep at least one).
  • Text shadow (Style tab): None, Soft, Strong or Hard, in black, main, accent or white. Hard with Accent or White pops most on a dark background.
  • Photo tab: add a background photo. While this tab is open, drag the preview to move the photo and pinch to zoom, from 30% to 400%. Below 100% it sits smaller, with your background color around it. Photo strength tints it in team color.
  • Moving pieces: on the Graphic, Style and Layers tabs, drag any piece on the preview to move it and pinch to resize. It snaps to the center lines.
  • Layers tab: tap a piece name for its size and opacity sliders, the arrow nudge pad (1, 5 or 20 points per tap; hold to repeat), Center, Delete and Reset.
  • Tap + Text for your own words (SENIOR NIGHT, a hashtag) or + Image for a sticker or second logo. Select added text to change its wording, color or font.
  • Align: select any piece with text and pick Left, Center or Right to line it up. Auto keeps the design's own layout. Long venues wrap into two even lines on their own.
  • Text color: select any piece with text (team name, score, scorers, venue...) and pick a color, or type any hex code. Kit colors puts it back. Save layout to kit to keep it.
  • Remove background: tap a sponsor logo in the kit editor, select the Logo or an added image in Layers, or use the button under Team logo in Settings. Slide Strength until the edges look clean.
  • Save layout to kit keeps the arrangement and added items, so that graphic opens the same way next time. Reset all puts everything back.

Following a game live

  • On a shared team, the phone that kicks off keeps score. Everyone else on the team sees that game on their Game tab: clock, score, who is on, minutes, goals, cards and the timeline, updating as it happens.
  • If you tap Kick off while someone else is keeping score, the app asks: follow their game, take over scoring, or keep score here anyway (for a different game, like JV at the same time).
  • Take over scoring: if the scorekeeper's phone dies or they leave at halftime, the Manager or a Scorekeeper can ask to take over from the live view. The scorekeeper's phone gets Allow / Deny. If it doesn't answer in 15 seconds, or it has gone quiet, the take-over goes through. You pick up right where the game is, and their phone switches to following. The team owner can always take over without asking.
  • Take-overs need approval (Settings > Scorekeeping, owner only): take-overs wait for the scorekeeper or the owner to tap Allow, with no 15-second fallback.
  • While another phone keeps score, the roster and team settings are locked on the other phones until the game ends, so two phones can't change them at once. The game report notes who kept score and any hand-offs.
  • Put your name in Settings › Account & sync so others see who is keeping score.
  • Stop following hides the live view so you can set up your own game. Follow live brings it back.
  • Following needs a connection. If the scorekeeper loses signal, the live view says when it last updated and catches up when they reconnect.

Account, backup and sharing

  • Settings › Account & sync › Sign in with Apple or email. Signing in is optional, but it backs up every team with players or games to your account, free. Delete the app or get a new phone, sign in again, and your teams come back.
  • Share this team (FinalSheet Pro) uploads your roster, season, brand kits, photos and team settings. From then on they sync on their own.
  • Sharing a team is Pro, for the Manager who runs it. Scorekeepers and parents join free with the invite code and get the team's Pro features there.
  • End of season (Settings › End of season): Archive makes the team read-only and moves it to Past seasons in Your teams. Its season, reports and graphics stay to look back on, everyone on it keeps seeing it, and it no longer counts toward the free version's teams. The Manager can un-archive it, or delete it for good (offered the season CSV first).
  • If the Manager's Pro ends, the team is paused: everyone stays on it and can see the roster and games, but no one else can keep score, take over, follow live or join until the Manager has Pro again.
  • To share the team, send the invite code to your assistants and players' families. They sign in on their phone, tap I have an invite code and enter it.
  • Roles: everyone joins as a Follower. Followers follow games live, see the season and reports, and make share graphics, but can't change anything. The Manager makes assistants Scorekeepers under Settings > Members.
  • Scorekeepers keep score, edit the roster, games and film links, and change brand kits. Only the Manager changes the team name, logo, colors and game rules, deletes games, manages Members, and can take over scoring without asking.
  • Members: the Manager can change anyone's role, remove someone, or hand off the team to another member (you become a Scorekeeper). Anyone but the Manager can leave a team from Account & sync.
  • Each team gets its own space on the phone: joining one doesn't touch what's already there. Finish any game in progress first.
  • More than one team (say, a son's and a daughter's): tap the team name at the top to switch, or + New team to start an empty one. No sign-in needed: a new team is kept on this phone. Signed in, it's backed up to your account; with Pro you can also share it with its invite code. Each team keeps its own roster, season, kits and photos, so switching is instant and works offline. You can't switch while keeping score in a live game. Press and hold a phone-only team to delete it.
  • The free version keeps 2 teams you run; Pro keeps as many as you like. Teams you joined with an invite code don't count.
  • One phone keeps score for each game: the first one to kick off. The finished game goes to the team when that phone taps Finish, and edits to it afterward sync too.
  • No signal? Keep going. Changes wait on the phone and sync when there is a connection. Settings shows what is waiting.
  • Appearance, Keep screen awake, Confirm opponent goals and Track shots & saves are each phone's own choice and don't sync.
  • New phone: sign in, then tap Use next to your team.
  • Sign out keeps everything on the phone. Delete account removes it from the cloud.

Season

  • Film: open a game and tap + Add video link to paste a YouTube, Hudl, Veo or Google Drive link, with a label like Full game or Highlights. Tap it to watch; ••• to rename or remove. Links sync to the team and are listed in the report.
  • The table shows games played, minutes, goals, assists and points for every player.
  • % avail. is minutes played out of minutes in games the player was available for.
  • The Games heading shows goals for, goals against and goal difference (GF · GA · +/-). Each game shows its own +/- after the score.
  • Tap a game for its minutes, then Report (PDF), Report text, CSV, Graphic or Delete.
  • Share season CSV opens cleanly in Excel or Google Sheets.

Settings

  • Help & how-to at the top opens this guide, same as the ? button in the header.
  • Team logo: a square image for the app header, the PDF report and share graphics. Remove background clears a plain background around it.
  • Team banner: a wide image across the top of the PDF report. The middle of your photo is used.
  • Team name, level and a short name (up to 4 letters) appear on the scoreboard and reports.
  • Social posts (Settings › your team): add your team's handles and hashtags, like @yourteam #gameday. They're added to the recap at the end of the text report.
  • Storage: see how much space goal clips and highlight reels use, delete clips that are already in a reel, or delete all videos. Games, stats and reports aren't affected.
  • Team colors: tap a color to pick from the palette, or type a hex code like #1d4aa8. They're used on social graphics (free) and the PDF report.
  • Appearance: Match my phone, Light or Dark. Dark is easier on the eyes at night games.
  • Points formula: Goal 2 + Assist 1, or Goal 1 + Assist 1.
  • Keep screen awake stops the phone from locking while the clock runs.
  • Confirm opponent goals asks before adding one, so a stray tap does not count, and lets you mark it a PK goal.
  • PKs: after your goal's scorer, pick PK (penalty kick) instead of an assist. Fix any goal later with Edit on the timeline.
  • Fouls: 🚩 [team] foul asks who committed it; 🚩 [opponent] foul just counts. Cards are still recorded with Card.
  • Penalty kicks during the game (turn on Show penalty kicks in Settings > On the sideline; the row also appears once a PK is recorded): 🥅 [team] PK asks who took it, then Scored, Saved or Missed. Scored adds a PK goal; Saved also counts as a shot and a save. The shootout after the game is separate.
  • Corner kicks: ⛳ [team] corner asks who took it; ⛳ [opponent] corner just counts. They show on the scoreboard, in reports and on the Season tab (CK).
  • Track game stats (off unless turned on) (Settings > On the sideline): from the next kickoff, 🎯 Shot records a shot on goal by your team that their keeper saved (pick the shooter), and 🧤 Save records a save by your keeper. Goals count as shots on goal on their own. Saves go to whoever is in the GK spot. Totals show on the scoreboard, in reports and on the Season tab.
  • Formations: pick one above the field. The Manager can tap + Custom and type a shape like 4-1-3-2; the app labels the spots and you can rename any of them. Custom formations sync to the team.

Your data

  • Everything is saved on this phone, and it survives closing the app.
  • Signed in, your teams are also backed up to your account. Signed out, they're on this phone only: deleting the app deletes your roster, photos, logo, banner, brand kits and games, so sign in (or share your season CSV now and then).
  • Goal clips and highlight reels are kept only on this phone. They are not backed up when you sign in, so share the ones you want to keep.
  • Clear season (in Settings) deletes all saved games but keeps your roster.

Quick fixes

  • Kick off is grey. Exactly 11 must be on the field. Check the count beside On field.
  • Wrong goal, card or sub. Tap Undo right away, or tap the goal or card to fix it. Press and hold one in its list to remove it.
  • Forgot Kick off or Finish on time. Fix Kickoff and Final whistle in Game info.
  • Referees missing from the report. Under Officials, pick the crew size first, then type the names.
  • PDF won't share. Use Share report text and paste it into a message.
  • Graphic text hard to read. On the Style tab, turn on Text shadow (Hard + Accent or White pops most), or lower Photo strength.
  • Goal clip plays black on Android. It is usually an iPhone HDR video. On the iPhone, share it as Most Compatible (or turn off HDR in Camera settings), then add it again.
  • FinalSheet takes too much space. Videos are the only big files. Go to Settings › Storage to delete clips already in a reel, or all videos.
  • Discarded a game by mistake. It can't be recovered. Always end with Finish.
