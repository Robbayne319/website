Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

FinalSheet keeps your team's roster, games and photos on your phone, and in the cloud only if you sign in. Only you, and the members of any team you share, can see it. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Who FinalSheet is for

FinalSheet is a sideline app for coaches, team managers and parents who track soccer games. It is meant to be used by adults.

The players it tracks are often minors. FinalSheet does not collect information from children: adults enter it about the players on their team. If you are under 13, do not create an account. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

FinalSheet does not ask for players' birth dates, addresses, phone numbers, school records or health information. It does not use your location or contacts.

Where it's stored and who can see it

  • Without signing in, your team's data stays on your phone. Nothing about your team is sent to us.
  • If you sign in, your teams are backed up to the cloud so they can be restored on a new phone. If you share a team or join one, its data also syncs between the phones of its members. It is stored with Supabase, our database provider.
  • Only members of a team can see its data. You join a team only with its invite code. The database itself blocks anyone who is not a member, including other FinalSheet users.
  • Live game following sends the game in progress to the members of that team only, while it is being played.
  • Reports and graphics leave the app only when you choose to share or save them. After that, where they go is up to you.
  • Photo background removal happens on your phone. The photo is not sent anywhere for it.
  • Crash reports are sent when the app hits an error, so it can be fixed. They do not include your name, email, roster or game data, and they are not linked to your account.

We use a few service providers to run the app: Apple (Sign in with Apple, TestFlight and the App Store), Supabase (accounts and cloud storage for shared teams), Resend (emailing sign-in codes), Sentry (crash reports) and Expo (delivering app updates). They handle data only to provide those services.

What FinalSheet does not do

  • No advertising, in the app or using your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics that profile you or your players.
  • No selling or renting of any data, to anyone.
  • No use of your team's data for anything other than running FinalSheet for your team.
  • Crash reports are used only to find and fix bugs.

Player photos, graphics and social media

Adding player photos is optional. Because most players are minors, whoever adds a photo is responsible for having permission from the player or a parent or guardian, and for following their school's or club's media policy. The app reminds you of this the first time you add a player photo.

Photos are used for the roster, game reports and the share graphics made in the app. Inside FinalSheet, they are visible only to members of your team.

Graphics and reports are made to be shared. When you or another member of your team shares or saves one, for example posting a goal graphic to Instagram, Facebook or X, sending it in a group chat or printing a report, it can show player names, numbers, photos and stats to anyone who sees it there. Whoever posts or sends it is responsible for having permission to share it publicly. The app reminds you of this the first time you share a graphic that shows a player's photo.

FinalSheet does not post anything on its own, does not connect to your social media accounts, and does not publish your graphics anywhere. Once a graphic or report has been shared, it is out of FinalSheet's control: removing a photo or player in the app does not remove copies that were already posted or sent. To take one down, delete it where it was posted, or ask the person who posted it.

Parents or guardians who don't want their child's photo used can ask the team's Manager to remove it, or contact us and we will remove it from the team's cloud copy within 30 days.

Deleting your data

  • Remove a player, photo, game or brand kit in the app, and it is removed from your phone and cleared from the cloud copy for your whole team.
  • Delete account (Settings, then Account & sync) removes your account from the cloud. Teams that only you belong to are deleted with all their data. Teams shared with other members stay with them.
  • Sign out stops syncing but leaves your data on your phone. Deleting the app removes the data on that phone.
  • The live game view keeps a team's most recent game until the team starts its next one.
  • Ask us to delete a player's information or a team's data, and we will do it within 30 days. A parent or guardian can ask too; we may first check with the team's Manager that the request is genuine.

Security, changes and contact

Data sent to the cloud travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in uses Sign in with Apple or a one-time code emailed to you, so FinalSheet never sees or stores a password. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your team's invite code within your team and make a new one if it gets out.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be noted in the app.
