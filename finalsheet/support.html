Support

We usually reply within a day or two

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email linesandbars@gmail.com. A screenshot and the steps that led to it help a lot.

The app also has a full guide: tap the ? at the top of any screen.

Getting the beta

  • Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone.
  • Open the beta link from the Download section on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install.
  • New versions show up in TestFlight; small fixes arrive on their own the next time you open the app.

Sharing a team with other coaches

  • On your phone: Settings › Account & sync, sign in with Apple, then Create team from this phone. You'll get an invite code.
  • Other coaches sign in on their phones, tap I have an invite code and enter it. Joining replaces that phone's roster and season with the team's.
  • On game day the phone that kicks off keeps score. Everyone else on the team can follow the game live, and any coach can take over scoring.

Common questions

  • Do I have to sign in? No. Everything works on your phone without an account. Signing in is only for backup and sharing a team.
  • No signal at the field? Keep going. The game is saved on your phone and syncs when you're back online.
  • Will I lose my data when I update? No. Updating keeps everything. Deleting the app removes what's on that phone, so sign in to a team first if you want a cloud copy.
  • A goal or card is wrong. Tap Undo right away, or tap the goal or card in the game (or on the timeline) to edit it, even after the game is finished.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings › Account & sync › Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
Privacy: your team's data is visible only to your team, with no ads or tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
