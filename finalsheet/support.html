Sharing a team

Sharing a team is part of FinalSheet Pro, for the Manager who runs the team. Scorekeepers and parents join free.

  • Manager: Settings › Account & sync, sign in with Apple or with email, then tap Share this team and send the invite code to your assistants and players' families.
  • Everyone else: sign in on your phone (Android phones use email), tap I have an invite code and enter it. The team gets its own space on that phone, so joining doesn't touch any team that's already there.
  • Roles: everyone joins as a Follower, who can follow games, see the season and reports, and make graphics. The Manager makes helpers Scorekeepers under Settings › Members & roles.
  • On game day the phone that kicks off keeps score and everyone else follows live. If the scorekeeper's phone dies or they leave, the Manager or a Scorekeeper can ask to take over, and the scorekeeper's phone gets Allow / Deny.

Common questions

  • Do I have to sign in? No. FinalSheet works on your phone without an account. Signing in backs up every team to your account for free, and you need it to share a team.
  • I coach more than one team. Tap the team name at the top to switch, or + New team to start another. Each team keeps its own roster, season and brand kits.
  • My sign-in code didn't arrive. Check your spam or junk folder, then tap Send a new code. Make sure the email address is spelled right.
  • No signal at the field? Keep going. The game is saved on your phone and syncs when you're back online.
  • Will I lose my data when I update? No. Updating keeps everything. Deleting the app removes what's on that phone, so sign in first and your teams come back when you sign in again.
  • How do I add a goal clip? Film the play with your Camera app. After you log the goal, tap it, then Choose video and pick the clip (up to 30 seconds). Clips stay on your phone.
  • How do I make a highlight reel? After the game, open it in Season and tap Make wide 16:9 or Make story 9:16. You can keep the sound or leave it out.
  • FinalSheet is using a lot of storage. Videos are the only big files. See Settings › Storage to delete clips that are already in a reel, or all videos. Games and stats aren't affected.
  • A goal or card is wrong. Tap Undo right away, or tap the goal or card in the game (or on the timeline) to edit it, even after the game is finished.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings › Account & sync › Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
