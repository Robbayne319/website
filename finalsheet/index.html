Features

More than a minutes clock

FinalSheet keeps the whole match in one place, so nothing lives on a clipboard or in your head after the game.

01

Live minutes

A running clock for every player on the field. Stop it for stoppages and halftime; minutes stay accurate to the second.

02

One-tap subs

Tap who comes off and who goes on. Each sub is logged to the match minute and minutes adjust automatically.

03

Field view

Pick a formation and drag players into position. Works for 11v11, 9v9 and 7v7.

04

Goals, assists & cards

Log the scorer, the assist and every card with its reason, then fix any of it after the game.

05

PDF game report

Score, events, minutes, officials, weather and a timeline of the whole match, ready to send at full time.

06

Social graphics

Final score, goal, live score and starting-lineup graphics in your colors, fonts and sponsor logos.

07

Follow live

One phone keeps score; the rest of your staff and parents follow the game live, and any coach can take over.

08

Season stats

Games played, minutes, goals, assists, points and availability add up game by game.

09

Game film

Add YouTube, Hudl or Veo links to each game, so the film lives with the report.

How it works

Kickoff to final sheet in three steps

1

Set your lineup

Add your roster once. Before each game, pick your starters, the opponent and the date.

2

Tap as it happens

Start the clock. Log subs, goals, assists and cards with a tap. Minutes count themselves.

3

Share the final sheet

At full time, send a clean game report and graphics to players, parents, your athletic director or the press.

The game report

The whole match on one sheet

Final score, scorers and assists with match minutes, cards with reasons, every substitution, a timeline of the game and each player's time on the field.

  • Share as a PDF, by text or email
  • Season totals build up game by game
  • Fair playing-time records you can stand behind

Reliable records

Numbers you can stand behind

When a parent asks about playing time or the athletic director needs season totals, the answer is already on the sheet.

Every event timestamped

Subs, goals and cards are logged to the match minute, so minutes are calculated, not estimated.

Fix mistakes fast

Tapped the wrong player? Undo it, or correct a goal or card after the game.

Shared with your staff

Sign in to share the roster, season and graphics with the rest of your coaches. Only your team can see it.

Works without signal

No Wi-Fi at the field? Tracking keeps going, and your team syncs when you're back online.
