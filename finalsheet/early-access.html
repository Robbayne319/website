FinalSheet is game-day software for soccer team managers and coaches, from youth and club teams to high school and college. Track every minute from the sideline, keep parents updated live, and post the final score before you reach the parking lot. The coach coaches. You've got the rest.

We're opening a limited number of early access spots ahead of the spring season. Early adopters get:

  • Full Pro, free through the spring season. Saved games, season stats, game and season reports, team sharing and custom graphics.
  • Hands-on setup. We'll help you load your roster, team colors and schedule, so game day just works.
  • A direct line to the product team. Your feedback shapes what we build next.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Early adopters get every Pro feature at no cost through the spring season. No card needed.

Does my coach need to apply?

No. Once you're set up, share your team with an invite code. Your coach, assistants and parents join free and see everything live.

What's TestFlight?

Apple's official app for early-access software. You install TestFlight from the App Store, then FinalSheet installs through it like any other app.

Who's behind FinalSheet?

FinalSheet is built by Moxie App Design, a software studio in Pennsylvania. Questions? Visit Support.
