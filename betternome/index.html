The metronome

Fast to set, hard to lose.

A big display you can read from the drum stool, and controls that do one thing each.

Tap & drag

Set it by feel

Tap the display to count a tempo in, or drag up and down to dial it. Halve or double it in one press.

Time

Any time signature

From 4/4 to 7/8 with your own grouping. Tap a beat to make it a downbeat, an accent or plain.

Sub & sound

Hear what you need

Quarters, 8ths, triplets or 16ths, on a beep, woodblock, rimshot, cowbell, hi-hat, kick or snare, with its own volume.

Train

Practice modes

Ramp the tempo up as you go, drop bars of silence to test your time, or let it drop random beats.

Flash

See the beat

Turn on the flash to follow the pulse with the sound off, or when it's too loud to hear a click.

Setlists

Every song ready

Save each song's tempo and time signature to a setlist and step through them on the gig.

Analyze a song

Load a song. Get the tempo, the loop and the MIDI.

Choose an MP3, WAV, M4A or AIFF, or play it out loud and record with the mic.

  • BPM you can trust. See how confident the read is, whether the song drifts, and the tempo over time. Hear it a different way? Pick the pulse.
  • Loop a section. Set A and B and play the hard bar over and over, with the click locked to the song.
  • Match the song to your tempo. Slow it down to learn it, keeping the pitch or like tape, then tap or drag the display and the song follows.
  • Beat to MIDI. Betternome finds the kick, snare and hats and writes them out as a MIDI file you can drop into Logic or any DAW.
  • Stays on your phone. Songs and recordings are analyzed right on your device and never uploaded.

With an Apple Music subscription, search a song and play it right inside Betternome, loop a section, and keep the click with it.
