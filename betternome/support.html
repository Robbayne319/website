Support

We usually reply within a day or two

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email linesandbars@gmail.com. For a wrong tempo or MIDI, attaching the song or a short clip helps a lot.

Getting the iPhone beta

  • Email us and we'll send a TestFlight invite.
  • Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the invite link and tap Accept, then Install.
  • New builds show up in TestFlight.

Using the web app

  • Open moxieappdesign.com/betternome/app in Safari or Chrome.
  • To keep it on your Home Screen, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Apple Music, song search and Spotify are only in the iPhone app. Everything else works in the browser.

Common questions

  • I can't hear the click. Check your ringer switch and volume, and the Click volume slider. Turn on Flash to see the beat.
  • The BPM is half or double what I expect. Tap ÷2 or ×2, or use Feel it differently? Pick the pulse after analyzing a song.
  • Record with mic doesn't start. Allow microphone access for Betternome (or for your browser) in Settings, then try again. Hold the phone near the speaker.
  • A drum is named wrong in the MIDI. Under Beat to MIDI, tap ▶ to hear each sound and change its name, then save again. Sensitivity and Snap to grid help with busy mixes.
  • Apple Music won't play. Playing songs in Betternome needs an active Apple Music subscription, and permission under Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music.
  • A song search has no BPM. Not every song has a tempo in the catalog. Play the song and use Record with mic instead.
  • Will I lose my setlists? Updating keeps them. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data, removes them.
